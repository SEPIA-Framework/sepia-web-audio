Volume-Processor

Amplifies the microphone signal by 'Gain' and shows its level on the meter.

Resampler (Speex WASM)

Converts the audio to the target sample rate with speexdsp and passes it on in chunks of 'Buffer-Size' samples to the modules after it.

Basic Buffer

Collects the audio into chunks of 'Buffer-Size' samples at the original rate, for the modules after it; used when the resampler is off.

VAD

Detects speech and reports when an utterance starts and ends; here it also opens and closes the wave encoder's recording.

Wake-Word

Listens for a keyword while its gate is open, with Porcupine or the SEPIA KWS as chosen above.

Wave Encoder

Records the audio while its gate is open and turns it into a WAV file; 'Lookback' adds the audio from just before the gate opened.

Media Recorder

Records with the browser's own MediaRecorder in a codec the browser offers; used when the wave encoder is off.
